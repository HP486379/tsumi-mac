Mac mini M1 / M2 / M4 — common-scale reference prototype

The game keeps its zero-gap collision-aligned models. This prototype shows the requested real proportions without stretching; applying them at the current physical centers would reintroduce gaps. Design approval is required.

Reference mesh: 1 logical px = 1 mm on all axes. Identical camera and panel scale. M4 front ports follow Apple 2024 specifications; corner radii are illustrative, not CAD measurements.
